Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This app is a companion: a cat that lives on your iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short, everything stays on your devices.

What the app stores

The cat you chose and the name you gave it, your chat with the cat, its diary, when you answered its meal and water reminders, and your settings. All of this is stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in your own device backups if you use them. We run no servers and receive none of it.

Health data

With your permission, the app reads these items from Apple Health: step count, exercise minutes, heart rate, and activity rings. They are used only to decide what the cat is doing and saying, such as exercising along with you or celebrating when your rings are closed.

Health data is read on your device. It is never sent anywhere, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising. The app does not write to Apple Health. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

The app is not a medical device. What the cat says about your heart rate or activity is not medical advice.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.

What the app does not do

There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party software in the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how information is handled, for example by offering an online chat feature, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the app will ask you first.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the app's App Store page.
